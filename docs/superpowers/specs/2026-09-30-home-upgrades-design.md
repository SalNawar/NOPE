# Home upgrades: the House tree (design)

*2026-09-30 · Saleh answered Q1-Q6 on 2026-09-29 (§10: all decided; HU6, HU7, HU10, §2's top tier, §4, §9 and §12 follow his answers, `fix/home-balance`) · drafted by Claude from Saleh's direction of 2026-09-29 and his design rules of the same day · read-only map of `main` `e30184c` and of the Orders track's branch `feat/orders` at `06e4883` (not on `main` yet) · the code wins over this text · it extends the portals spec v3 (`origin/design/portals`, `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-30-portals-design.md`, **S**): its HM1-HM3 and §7 (four house upgrades) are the starting point, and this spec replaces §7's list and HM2 with a larger tree.*

Saleh, verbatim (2026-09-29, relayed by the orchestrator):

> "way more than four and go into upgrading not just sickness: there is food, better housing, security; add more money sinks"

The work upgrades (scanners, translators, protocols, contacts, portal repairs) move to the PC's Orders app (the Orders track, S OR1). **Home keeps the expenses, family care, the slot machine and sleep, and gains the House tree.**

Saleh's design rules (2026-09-29) apply; rule 4 matters most here: every price, effect and prerequisite is data he edits in the engine (the content spreadsheet for the tree, the Inspector for the household knobs).

## 2. The tree (the first cut; every number is a spreadsheet cell)

Prices are set against phase 23's economy: perfect play at the shift clock's pace nets about 15 cr a day after the household's 60 cr, so a careful clerk affords the cheap comforts early; upkeep makes every food plan and subscription a nightly choice. ## 3. Effect ops (appended to `EffectOpType`, pinned from now on)

All continuous (summed while active, `EffectOps.ActsWhileActive` true, so a dialog's effect may not carry them), in this order after `SetFact` (17): `HouseholdExpense` (cr a night on rent and utilities), `SicknessChance` (+chance a member worsens a night), `CareCost` (cr on a treatment), `Upkeep` (cr a night, its own bill line), `MedicalDrain` (cr on each condition point), `Mood` (points), `BreakInChance` (+chance), `BreakInShare` (+share of the wallet taken). If the Orders or portals track appends first, ours are renumbered after theirs.

## 4. The household knobs (Inspector, `GameConfig_Default`; Generate World never writes it)

Kept: `baseDailyExpense` 30, `expensePerFamilyMember` 15, `expensePerConditionPoint` 1, `conditionCareCost` 8, `conditionWorsenChance` 0.25, `maxFamilyCondition` 10, `slotSpinCost` 10. New: `recoveryPerMood` 0.03, `maxRecoveryChance` 0.4, `sicknessPerMood` 0.01, `maxMoodSicknessCut` 0.1 (Saleh's Q3), `breakInFromDay` 11 (Q2), `breakInChance` 0.08, `breakInShare` 0.25, `breakInMaxLoss` 60 (Q2 kept them).

## 5. The evening

1. **Expenses** (Home opens, `DayCycle.OpenHome(world, lib, config, daySeed)`, in this order so a reload replays it): the break-in roll, the bill (rent and utilities, family, medical drain, upkeep, the break-in's loss), the night's drift and recovery. The panel lists the lines, the mood in words (never a number), tonight's changes and the family rows with Treat (the care cost with the house effects).
2. **House** (the tree, §6). "Bought tonight: in force from tomorrow night."
3. **Slot** (unchanged).
4. **Sleep**: with the Radio owned, the panel adds one radio line (`home.radio[]`, the day's line by `day % count`, no random draw).

The statement (TC-960): household costs = the bill including the break-in, plus care; purchases = house upgrades and spins (unchanged columns).

## 7. Saves

Nothing new: a house upgrade is an owned upgrade (`unlockedUpgradeIds`) and its effect an active effect entry; mood, break-ins and recovery are recomputed from them and the day seed. The save version stays.

## 8. Tests (EditMode, Domain first)

`HomeRulesTests` (Adjusted, Cost rounding and floor, MoodShare cap, WorsenChance with the house and the mood, Recovers pinned to its stream, Night's order, BreakIn pinned, BreakInLoss: positive wallet only, share, cap); `SeedsTests` (the two salts distinct and pinned; each stream apart from every other); `SerializedEnumsTests` (`EffectOpType` pinned with the eight new members; `UpgradeBranch` with Home's five); `EffectOpsTests` (the new ops act while active); `UpgradeTreeTests` (Layout for Home's venue: only Home's, a band per category, a chain in its slot; BranchesOf; Problems allows a Home prerequisite at Home and refuses one at Orders or a branch of the other venue); `ContentSheetMapTests` (the `home` sheets round-trip); `HousePolicyTests` (the reserve, the cheapest buyable, nothing when nothing is buyable, care at the threshold; the climber: the cheapest top-tier path, all of it or nothing, a shared prerequisite once); `BribePolicyTests` (every offered dialog's first paying choice, nothing else); `HouseEffectsTests` (the words; no digit or % for a chance, share or the mood, over a sweep of values).

## 9. The balance simulation's buying policy

`BalanceSimulation` runs each style four times more at Home, after the bills each night: care first (a member at condition 3 or more, while the wallet covers it and stays above the reserve), then the **buyer** buys the cheapest buyable house upgrade while the wallet stays above `houseReserve` after the price (one purchase a night), or the **climber** (Saleh's Q6) buys nothing but the cheapest top-tier path (an upgrade from `topTierPrice` with its missing prerequisites), all of it on the night the wallet covers the whole path and keeps the reserve; each shopper once as a careful clerk and once as one who **takes the bribes** (`BribePolicy`: every dialog offered at the desk with a choice whose effect pays is finished on that choice; today that is Rook's, day 11, `Effect_Dialog_BribeTaken`, applied at the shift's close as the game applies a dialog's effect). The summary adds, per style, pace and variant: upgrades bought (mean count, each one's runs and mean day), upkeep paid, break-ins and their losses, care paid, the bribes taken, the top tier reached (runs with one, with two or more, the first's mean day), and the wallet's lowest point and the end's mean and range, next to the plain runs; and Saleh's Q6 as one table (the top tier by day 15, careful against bribe-taking).

## 10. Saleh's answers (2026-09-29; the questions were provisional until then)

1. **Where the tree's numbers live.** Kept: the content spreadsheet (`homeUpgrades` sheet, Generate World writes the assets).
2. **The break-in.** "it will start mid second week": from day 11 (`breakInFromDay`, an Inspector knob; week one is days 1-7), 8 % a night, a quarter of a positive wallet up to 60 cr (kept).
3. **Mood's effect.** "both but dont reveal numbers to the player": mood lowers the chance a member gets worse and raises a sick member's chance to recover, each a little weaker than the recovery-only effect (HU7); no chance, percentage or mood number anywhere in the player's view (the House's cards and detail, the expenses, the sleep panel), words instead.
4. **Upkeep when the wallet is short.** Kept: billed like rent (the wallet may go negative; bankruptcy decides).
5. **Selling back or downgrading.** Kept: no, a purchase is final.
6. **Prices.** "balance so you can buy one or two if you take bribes": the top tier is priced so a careful clerk at the shift clock's pace reaches none of it by day 15 and one who takes the bribes reaches one (§2, §12). Decided on Saleh's behalf while he slept (the coordinator's instruction to take the recommended way): main pays one bribe, Rook's 50 cr on day 11, and a careful perfect clerk's own savings by day 15 already range over some 220 cr between runs, so no price can put the top tier beyond every careful clerk and within reach of one who took 50 cr; **the bribe is raised to 200 cr** (`Effect_Dialog_BribeTaken`'s `AddMoney`, an Inspector knob, and the five lines that name it: Rook's offer, the choice, his reply, the complaint and the audit). More bribe beats ("if you take bribes", plural) are content for a later pass; each would add to `BribePolicy`'s take with no code change.
