## 2. Decisions (Saleh's to overturn)

1. **The hand is gone.** The desk-first redesign lifted a paper "into the hand" (two screen slots, a desk catcher, Escape's put-back, hold-to-pick). Papers, Please has no hand: the desk zone is the reading size. `PaperExaminer`, `ExamineLayout`, `HeldCover`, `PaperLanding` and their tests were deleted; a handed-over paper lands on a counter spot instead of a landing search.
2. ~~The stamps are a 2D bar on the overlay~~ (overturned the same day, §4.1: Saleh wants the 3D stamps). No ink pad and no pick-up stay gone: Papers, Please's stamps have no ink and are never carried. The thump and the thunk are generated in code (no sound asset yet; the request is in `docs/ART_ASSET_LIST.md`, "The desk").
3. **The verdict is the drop, not a button.** "Hand the papers back" buttons are gone; the counter's strip reads "COUNTER", then "▲ HAND BACK ▲" once the passport carries its verdict. The stamped passport handed back takes every paper with it (Papers, Please makes you hand each back; one drop is kept to stay one step).
4. **The PC key is Q** (E is free too; Q sits by TAB and SPACE for the left hand; A/D are the city). On the PC, Q goes back unless a text field types it; SPACE and TAB stay the PC's (Space picks the focused row, Tab walks the app's regions: they are its keyboard access), so "the same everywhere" means every *desk* state (normal view, reading view, inspect mode, stamp bar out, wheel open, city, a drag): each key first closes what is in its way (the wheel, the city).
5. **The PC stays a comparing tool without a mode**: on the PC a click on a value compares at once, as before; inspect mode is the desk's. A right-click on the PC runs the PC's Escape chain (closing a menu or the card, clearing the search, leaving a field, letting go of a held value), then closes the PC. The PC's right-click context menus moved to **Shift+F10 / the Menu key** (Copy, Copy row, Pin, Pick; an icon's Open; the desktop's Arrange icons); a pin unpins with Ctrl+P.
6. **The reading view stays** (the 80° tilt) as the desk zone's view: a document dropped on the desk, or the stamp bar slid out, brings it by itself; papers move in both views (the 2026-09-30 rule "papers move only while tilted" is retired: controls win).
7. **Inspect mode ends** with the PC frame, the wheel or a newsletter (it is the desk's); leaving it lets go of a held value. A click on the traveller in inspect mode compares their face; the intercom still opens the wheel.
8. **Knobs** (`DeskConfigSO`): `counterDepth` (was `handBackDepth`), `counterScale` 0.6, `readingHeight` 0.34 m, `counterSpots` 4 and `counterSpacing` 0.17 m (were `paperSpawnSlots` / `landingGrid`), `dragLift` (was `heldPaperLift`), `readingTint` (was `examineTint`), `stampBarSeconds` (was `stampTraySeconds`), `stampPressSeconds`, `stampNoteSeconds`; the tray's, the ink's and the examine pose's knobs are gone.

## 4. The desk fixes (Saleh after playing, 2026-10-06; `fix/desk-stamps-passport`)

Decided and built without questions; every choice is the default, Saleh's to overturn.

1. **The 3D stamps.** Saleh: "I want the 3D stamp; there should be a label on the screen to bring out the stamp stuff, and the Tab shortcut." The bar is the desk polish's stamps back as Papers, Please's bar: a rack (`Office/StampRack`) of the art's desk stamp model (DeskClean `Clean_Stamp` at half size in the desk's NOPE/Desk Anime materials: wooden handle, brass ferrule, a red or green cap, the word on the block's front and reversed on the rubber), DENIED left and APPROVED right (Papers, Please's order), their words also printed on the rail's top (readable from the steep reading view, where the block's front is not), the rail in front of the stamps (under them on the screen, so it never covers the passport whose visa box is under a stamp). The grey tab on the overlay's right edge ("STAMPS" over TAB), TAB and the desk's stamp prop slide it in from the desk's right (`DeskConfigSO.stampBarTravel` 0.6 m) to where the reading view shows the desk at `stampBarView` (0.72, 0.36); it hides when in. The dies hang `stampHover` 3 cm over the desk (above a dragged paper's 2 cm lift), so a paper slides under them. A stamp presses **what lies straight under its die** (a ray down from the die's centre, not the camera's ray: the physical rule, and the stamp's body hides the camera's view of the spot anyway); the press dips the stamp onto the paper. **No ink**: Papers, Please has none, so a click on a stamp is the whole action (the desk-first ink pad and dry press stay gone). The rulebook under a stamp refuses it like any other paper. One verdict per passport, the counter hand-back: unchanged (`StampFlow`).
2. **The plain passport.** Saleh: "there is a long number on the passport and many <<<<. ... let's simplify, look at the fields in Papers, Please." The machine-readable zone is gone (`MachineZone`, its tests, the block kind 18, the zone's metrics and text role deleted; 18 is never reused). Papers, Please's fields: name, date of birth, issuing office (the header's programme line, "Passport Office"), expiry, a short number, the photo, the nation. The game has no sex field: skipped. The nation's three-letter code (`countries[].passport.code`, the zone's before) now prints under the emblem in the header (the emblem shrinks to give it the room; nothing moves). The passport's number stays the **Citizen ID** (label unchanged) because the same value is compared on every paper and the record, so it keeps one name everywhere. Every compared agency number is short now: a Citizen ID "KTR-418" (three capitals without I and O, three digits; three draws as before, so the account stream keeps its order), a Displacement No. "DP-4471" (two draws as before), a waiver or policy number "SW-2048" / "TI-5519" (one draw). The incident ("R-0311-07", it carries the date) and the transponder serial ("HP-40718") were short already. The authored IDs in `world_source.json` (the clerk TMW-773, the story characters) are rewritten, Generate World and the validator check the new format (`Premades.IsCitizenId`), the content sheets' notes say it. The barcode serial under a form's barcode ("TC-230/583021") is no compared value (no rule, record or finding reads it) and is kept as print.
3. **Clipping.** Measured in the art office: the blotter's top is the desk plane (1.069 m); a dragged rulebook was put back on that plane exactly (z-fighting with the blotter), and lying 1 mm up its print (0.6 mm over the card) rose above the first paper's sheet (1.5 mm), so the rulebook's words showed through a passport laid on it. Fix: the rulebook takes a place in the papers' stack (`DeskController`, `DeskRulebook.SetLift` on its new Booklet child; the root stays on the plane where the drag moves it): one 1.5 mm step a place in the order things were last touched, a dragged rulebook lifted above everything like a dragged paper. A place's print is at most 0.8 mm deep, under the next step. The papers' and the rulebook's click boxes are 0.4 mm thin (they were 4 mm, scaled with the paper's zone), so the stack alone decides what a click or a stamp meets. Proven in both views by the probe's screenshots.
4. **The inspect line.** Saleh: "inspect mode thing stays after the result is made, the dash line." The desk's line is inspect mode's: dashed only while a value is held (it follows the pointer); a result draws a solid line and its label (a note's grey line is solid over the office too: `MatchLines.dashNotes` false there, so dashed never means anything but "holding"); the result stays until the player moves on (the next left-press that makes no new result, or a new value held: `MatchBoard.LineVersion`) and leaving inspect mode clears it (`MatchBoard.ClearLine`) with whatever was held; outside inspect mode nothing is drawn over the office.
5. **The magnifier.** The red inspect button's "!" is a white magnifying glass drawn in code (`MagnifierGlyph`: a lens ring over faint glass with a glint, a thick handle down to the right) over the key, SPACE; no sprite asset needed.
