# Home: the pet and the nightly bills (design)

*2026-10-05 · desk-first redesign, Track F (Saleh's item 14) · branch `feat/home-pet` from `main` `0e6cbd1` · built and decided by Claude without questions (Saleh: "no questions: decide, document") · it changes the Home upgrades spec (`2026-09-30-home-upgrades-design.md`): the family it cares for is now a pet; the House tree, its prices and the break-ins stay · the code wins over this text.*

Saleh, verbatim (2026-10-05):

> "in the home the player should have the choice on what to spend money on: food, heating, electricity, tv, medicine, each impacting the family members. I'm also thinking you choose to adopt a dog or a cat instead of a family and you need to take care of them; you get to name them and later on you will be able to see them and pet them and buy them toys"

His answer to the open question: **pet only for now**, the pet replaces the family in the demo.

## Decisions

| Id | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| PS1 | **Adoption at New Run.** New Run (from the title or an ending) opens the adoption panel on the Title: Dog or Cat (each drawn), a name field with the kind's suggested name (Biscuit, Miso), Adopt and start, Back. The name is trimmed and its spaces joined (`PetNames.Clean`), then refused when empty, longer than `home.pet.nameMaxLength` (16) or holding anything but letters of any script, spaces, hyphens and apostrophes (`PetNames.Check`; digits, symbols and rich-text tags are refused, so the name prints safely on the paper). A refusal says why under the field (`adopt.problem.*`) and adopts nothing. A run started anywhere else (the editor's office scene, the balance simulation) gets `RunConfigSO.startingPetKind` (Dog) with the suggested name (`DayCycle.Adopt`); an older save adopts that default on Continue (`WorldState.pet` is additive, no save bump). | "you get to name them"; validated so no name breaks the paper or the panels. |
| PS2 | **The night's bills.** Home opens with the fixed bill paid as before (rent and utilities with the House's HouseholdExpense ops, the sick pet's extra care per step of sickness with the MedicalDrain ops, the House's upkeep, the break-in). Then the bills step offers five optional bills, priced in `world_source.json` `home.bills` (the content spreadsheet's `homeBills`): **Food 12, Heating 9, Electricity 7, TV 4, Medicine 12** cr. Each row is Paying or Skip; the Heating and the TV only work with the Electricity, so choosing either chooses it and dropping the Electricity drops them (`PetCare.Toggle`). Medicine is offered only while the pet is unwell; its price carries the House's CareCost ops (Medicine Cabinet, Clinic). The step starts with food, heating and electricity chosen (medicine too when unwell; never the TV, `PetRules.DefaultCare`), or, when that is more than the wallet, with what the wallet covers in that order (`PetPolicy`). Pay is refused while the total is more than the wallet (nothing on credit; paying nothing is always allowed). | Saleh's five spending choices; "electricity powers heating and TV" made explicit in the UI instead of a trap. |
| PS3 | **Four needs, in words.** The pet has hunger, cold, boredom and sickness, each a level 0 (well) to `GameConfigSO.petNeedMax` (3). Tonight's care settles them (`PetRules.Settle`): fed → hunger 0, else a step up; warm (heating with power) → cold 0, else a step up; the TV (with power) and a toy played with each take boredom a step down, neither → a step up; medicine → sickness a step down. The player only ever reads words (`home.pet.hunger/cold/boredom/sickness`, best first: "Biscuit is very hungry.", `PetContent.Need`), never a number (tested). | Saleh's rule (Q3, 2026-09-29): "dont reveal numbers to the player". |
| PS4 | **Electricity on its own** powers the heating and the TV and the lights: without it the pet's corner is drawn dark ("The lights are off tonight."). It does nothing else for the pet. | Keeps electricity a real choice without a hidden effect. |
| PS5 | **Sickness at night.** At Sleep the pet's night runs on the day's seed (`HomeEconomy.PetNight`): the sickness roll reuses the household's stream (`HomeRules.Worsens`, place 0; `Seeds.ForFamily`) against `PetRules.SicknessChance` = `conditionWorsenChance` (0.15) + `sicknessPerNeed` (0.1) for each step of hunger and cold after tonight's care + the House's SicknessChance ops − the mood's share (the House's and the toys' Mood ops less the pet's boredom; `sicknessPerMood`, `maxMoodSicknessCut` as before); a sick pet also rolls its recovery (`HomeRules.Recovers`, `PetRules.RecoveryChance`); medicine tonight means no worse tonight. The next Home says what changed ("Biscuit caught something while you were at work."). | Neglect has consequences; the House's food, health and comfort upgrades keep their meaning (they now keep the pet well). |
| PS6 | **The failure state: the Animal Welfare Office.** A night that ends with hunger, cold or sickness at the worst brings the Welfare Office's notice (taped to the door, on the bills step, and a line in the next morning's paper); a second such night in a row (`GameConfigSO.welfareNights`, 2; 0 never) and the inspector rehomes the pet: the run ends on **"The Welfare Office"** (`EndingConditionType.PetTaken`, a failure, priority 80 under Fired 100 and Bankrupt 90; `Assets/Data/Endings/Ending_PetTaken.asset`, its body filled with the pet's name and kind). Non-graphic: the pet goes to "a family on the solvent floors, where the heating never goes off"; nobody dies or runs away into danger. Boredom alone never brings the inspector. | Saleh asked for a fitting, non-graphic failure replacing the family's; a notice first gives the careful player one night to fix it. |
| PS7 | **The pet's corner.** After the bills: the pet as tonight's care leaves it (`PetRules.Look`: sick, sad, happy or idle), drawn by `PetStandIn` from its art (`Home/pet_<kind>_<look>`, the corner `Home/pet_corner`) or, until the art arrives, a stand-in drawn in code (a body, head, ears that droop for a dog and stand for a cat, eyes, a tail that wags with its mood, a blanket when sick). **Pet** makes it hop with a heart and says the kind's next reaction line (no effect on its needs: petting is free affection, the money choices stay the levers). Each owned toy has a row: **Play** once a night (boredom a step lower tonight, a spin and the kind's toy line). | "see them and pet them and buy them toys". |
| PS8 | **Toys at the Orders app.** `home.toys` rows become Orders upgrades in a new `Toys` band (`UpgradeBranch.Toys` = 9, appended; the Orders tree's fifth band, its glyph a ball): Pre-owned Tennis Ball 20, Rope of Regulation Length 25, Feather Wand 30, Squeaky Auditor 45 cr; each a permanent Mood op (+1, the Auditor +2) once delivered the next morning, so owning toys keeps the pet well too. | "buy them toys" in the Orders app (the PC's shop), no parallel economy: the toys are upgrades with effect ops like the House's. |
| PS9 | **Money.** The bills replace the family's 2 × 15 cr: a careful clerk's essentials are 28 cr a night (58 with the rent, against 60 and the medical drain before), the TV 4 and medicine 12 when needed, so the House tree's tuning (Saleh's Q6: the top tier out of a careful clerk's reach, within a bribe-taker's) holds (§ Balance: a first cut at 24 cr let 15 of 50 careful climbers reach the top tier at the shift clock's pace; 28 cr brings it back to 1 of 50). The shift report's "tonight's bills" adds the essentials. The balance simulation pays the bills as a careful carer every night (`PetPolicy`: food, then heating with power, then medicine when unwell, each while the wallet covers it; the TV when bored and the reserve holds; a toy when owned) and reports the pet's bills, sick nights, unfed nights, the Welfare Office's notices and the pets taken. | "Rebalance the money so a careful clerk can keep the pet well; rerun the balance sim." |
| PS10 | **The paper.** The morning after the adoption the paper prints `home.pet.paperAdopted` ("SHELTER NEWS: Biscuit the dog has gone home with a clerk of Temporal Customs, Desk 3."); after a night left at the worst, `paperWelfare`. | "shown in the paper/Home". |
| PS11 | **Coats (2026-10-07, run 7).** Saleh: a very simple 2D dog and cat customization, one simple skin each, in the game's retro anime style, "4 or 5 options per animal". Each kind lists its coats in `home.pet.kinds[].coats` (an id and a UI string key for its name; the content sheet `homePetCoats`, the narrative workbook's pet block): the dog cream, tan, chocolate, charcoal, grey; the cat white, ginger, slate, black, cream. The adoption panel shows, after the kind, a row of its coats (small kit choice cards with the pet in that coat) and a big preview of the pet in the chosen coat, idle; the coat is saved (`PetState.coat`, additive: a save from before the coats gets the kind's first, `PetContent.CoatOf`) and every picture of the pet uses it (`ArtSlots.PetSprite`: `Home/pet_<kind>_<coat>_<look>`, then `Home/pet_<kind>_<look>`, then the code-drawn stand-in). **Decision:** the coat cannot be changed after the adoption (Saleh asked for customization at adoption only). The art: 40 pictures (2 kinds x 5 coats x 4 looks) made in Canva from one white-coated drawing per look and recoloured by `tools/art/pets/make_pets.py`, which also writes them into `Assets/Art/UI/Resources/Home` with fixed GUIDs. | Customization without a second system: one more saved id and one more art key. |

## Folded in, not parallel

- The family code is gone (`FamilyStateData`, the Treat rows, `HouseEffects.MoodLine`, `HousePolicy.Care`, `expensePerFamilyMember`, `maxFamilyCondition`, `conditionCareCost`, `RunConfigSO.startingFamilyMembers`, the family portraits' art slots); git history keeps it for a later "family or pet" choice.
- The House's ops keep their numbers and now act on the pet: SicknessChance and Mood on its nightly rolls, CareCost on the Medicine bill, MedicalDrain on the sick pet's extra care, HouseholdExpense on the rent, Upkeep and the break-in ops unchanged. Their words say so ("A little cheerier at home", "Medicine -3 cr"). Two blurbs that named "the kid" now name the pet.
- The evening is Bills → the pet's corner → House → Slot → Sleep.

## Balance (Tools > TimeDesk > Balance, 50 runs a style, 15 days)

"Before" is the last full run on the main line with the family (wave 5 track B's summary, 2026-09-30; the desk changes since move the pay only a little); "after" is this branch (the pet, the careful carer every night). At the shift clock's pace (10 travellers a shift), the plain runs:

| Style | Before: endings; wallet at day 15 mean (min-max); household a run | After: endings; wallet; household (of it the pet's bills); the pet |
|---|---|---|
| Perfect | world report ×50; 276 (142-366); 974 cr | world report ×50; 298 (151-366); 941 cr (469); sick 1.9 nights a run, never unfed, never taken |
| Imperfect | world report ×50; 156 (33-267); 971 cr | world report ×50; 181 (63-258); 938 cr (467); sick 1.9 nights, unfed 0.04, never taken |
| Careless | bankrupt ×44, fired ×6 | the pet taken ×30, fired ×20: a broke clerk cannot buy food on credit, so the pet goes before the wallet does |

Saleh's Q6, the top tier owned by day 15 at the pace, careful / takes the bribe: perfect climber before 0 (0 runs) / 0.98 (49), after 0.02 (1) / 1 (50); imperfect climber before 0 / 0.58 (29), after 0 / 0.78 (39); the buyer never reaches it careful, as before. The careful clerk's pet is never taken in any style or pace that keeps its job.

## Knobs (Inspector, `GameConfig_Default`)

`petNeedMax` 3, `conditionWorsenChance` 0.15, `sicknessPerNeed` 0.1, `welfareNights` 2, `expensePerConditionPoint` 1, and the mood knobs as before. Content (spreadsheet): `homeBills`, `homePet` (+ kinds, reactions, toy lines, the four needs' words), `homeToys`.
