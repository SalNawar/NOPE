Saleh, verbatim (2026-09-29), asked whether days 7 and after should keep replaying day 6's plan: **"Authored days 7-15"**.

His design rules of the same day, which override any spec text that disagrees:
1. One penalty for any wrong decision, approval or rejection alike, after one free warning a day that he can tune (`GameConfigSO.freeWarningsPerDay`, 1).
2. Personalities, not traveller types, drive claim lines, reactions and small talk; a believable but funny, absurd world.
3. Interactions are the same for every traveller type (the same wheel entries and verbs); only the reply changes.
4. Balance knobs are editable by Saleh in the Unity editor, and authored cases (premades, forced slots, story beats) stay outside the random draws.

And his follow-ups: premades keep their extra stability cost on a wrong call ("this is a different consequence"); stability becomes "much more resistant ... x.xx ... can compound" (phase 23 part 2, in flight on `redesign/p23-balance`: a compounding percentage, the firing line at 60 %).

## 2. The arc

### 2.1 The story

The second week is the **Debt Relief Drive**. On day 7 the Directorate announces that every Eligible account departs this quarter, and the clerk's own account (773-2840-19) reads Eligible. On day 8 the clerk of Desk 4, Ines Varga, comes through Desk 3 on her own Debt Relief departure; from day 9 her queue joins the clerk's. A rift storm floods the intake halls with the displaced, and Johannes Gutenberg is among them. A tour group on cheap transponders is lost in Babylonia, and on day 10 the agency grounds every Driftbox 3. A hustler offers the clerk 50 cr to read his contract quickly, and remembers what the clerk did with it; a 2150 enthusiast arrives dressed as Ada Lovelace; a stranger, Marek Hollis, arrives with a frozen account and the debt his father's waiver passed to him; and the Directorate's auditor tests the desk, and may already know about the fifty. Through it all Pell Quimby tries three times to leave for her honeymoon in Periclean Athens, and each week's new rule turns her away: an unsigned waiver, a recalled Driftbox, and from day 13 the range limit that grounds every cheap unit bound for the Ancient era. The week, and the run, end with the summer season, Pell's last try and the ending.
