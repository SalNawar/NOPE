## 1. Decisions

| Id | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | **A "▲ Back" control** at the top centre of the office overlay, right under the case HUD's strips (152 reference px down, 200 × 44), shows while the desk view is on; a click tilts back. It takes the "&lt; Desk" button's theme role (`DeskButton`: the same meaning, a way back). Its label is `deskView.back` ("▲ Back"); a hover hint on its right, `deskView.backHint` ("Esc / right-click"), in the tooltip's role (`HoverHint`). Both keys are Full tier (English, like the other desk notes). | The mat's click, Escape and the right-click worked but none of them showed. The control sits where the eye already looks for the claim, below the strips so it never covers them. The hint sits beside it, not under it: the bubble waits right under it in the desk view (§4.2). |
| B2 | **The mouse wheel.** Rolled up, it tilts back whenever the Back control shows. Rolled down with the pointer on the empty mat (the mat is the first thing under the pointer, so no UI, paper or prop is over it), it tilts in, when the mat's click would. | "Mouse wheel up also tilts back. | B3 | **The input table** (`BoothRules`) gains `DeskViewBackLive` = the desk view is on and the props are live (no frame, newsletter, wheel or stamp tray), **papers held or not**, and `DeskViewScrollInLive` = the view is normal and the mat's toggle is live. Tests first. | A way out must work while papers are held (the held papers re-pose to their slots in the normal view). The mat's own click keeps its one meaning (with papers held a desk click only puts them back). |
| B4 | The speech bubble and the wheel's ring, held on the screen while the traveller's head is above it (the desk view), keep the Back control's band clear too (204 reference px; it was 152). | They were kept below the strips only. |
| L1 | **Where a handed-over paper lands** (`PaperLanding`, Visuals, pure): each candidate spot is the lying paper's rectangle on the screen. A spot counts when the paper shows whole: inside the screen and under no cover. The covers are each held paper's places (its office slot, raised and dipped under the wheel: where it can sit while the new paper lies there), the case HUD's strips, the speech bubble and the wheel's ring (the desk's `landingCovers`, wired by the builder; each counts while shown). The spots are the spawn slots from the next in turn, then a fallback grid over the landing area (`DeskConfigSO.landingGrid`, 5 × 4, nearest the mat's centre first, never on the scanner). | "Pick the rule (for example, landing spots tested in screen space against the held slots, with a fallback)". | H1 | **The day-1 hints get a dark plate** (`NoteBacking`): a quad just behind the text, fitted to its drawn bounds plus 4 × 2 cm whenever the text changes, in a new unlit, transparent, dark material (`FloatingNote_Backing`, #121419 at 85 %, drawn before the text). Both hints ("Click the traveller to talk and ask for papers." and the scan note) have it. The wheel hint wraps onto two lines at twice its old size (a 1.2 × 0.23 m box, size up to 1). | It was about 12 px at 1080p, light on the pale morning crowds (1.2:1). A plate reads the same over the evening palette; the text keeps its outline. |
| C1 | **The audit** (a temporary job, §3): every text in the office overlay, the PC desktop (the frame open), Home and the Title, at 1920 × 1080, in 48 states, in the neutral theme and the eight culture themes (the leader forced as piece 6's checks do). UI text: the nearest stack of plain graphics behind it, composited until opaque. The PC frame's close and power buttons drop their empty labels (their glyphs are in the sprites). | "Fix at the source: theme palette roles, builder colours, the Home and Title builders, hardcoded colours. Prefer dark ink on light panels." The eight culture palettes are unchanged (they all pass the stricter classes). The Title needs nothing: its title and buttons are the art's (drawn in the sprites; their TMP texts are off), and the ending panel is dark with white text (passes). |

## 5. Self-review and intent audit

**Against Saleh's words.**
- *"there should be a way to back out of it"*: a visible "▲ Back" at the top of the screen whenever the view is tilted (papers held or not), with its other ways named on hover; the wheel backs out too, and rolls in over the mat. The existing ways stay.
- *"lots of text on many ui elements is not readable because the font is light color and the bg is light color"*: every text the game draws was measured in every state and theme that matters; all 21 texts below AA were fixed at their source (the Home rows were the white-on-cream he saw; the hints; the neutral Start and close; the paper labels), 0 remain; a builder check now refuses light-on-light.
- *"Third paper lands visibly"*: a handed-over paper lands where it shows whole, tested on the screen; when both slots hide the mat, the paper held longest goes back first.
- *"Day-1 hint gets a backing"*: both hints sit on a dark plate, readable in the morning and the evening, and the wheel hint is twice its old size.

**Redo and override checks.** The old landing rule is gone (grep: no `LandingSlot`, no `HeldCovers(`); the Home rows have no hardcoded white; the frame buttons have no empty labels; the neutral palette's Start and close colours are the builder's literals too. The mat's click, Escape, the right-click, NEXT's and the report's returns are unchanged (the play-through re-checked each). The culture palettes are unchanged; only the neutral theme's accent and reject moved. The art scene is byte-unchanged; the art's texts were only measured.

**No-code-path checks.** Every new behaviour is reached in the built scenes: the Back control and its hint (the builder wires them; the play-through clicks and hovers them), the wheel (a real virtual-mouse scroll), `PaperLanding` (every hand-over; the play-through's third paper), `NoteBacking` (both hints), the Home rows' ink and strip (the play-through), `UiContrastCheck` (every build of the office and Home). `DeskPapers.HeldLongest`, `PaperExaminer.ScreenRectOf`/`HeldPlaces` and `Contrast.ClassFor`/`WorstRatio` each have a production caller.

**Found and fixed during the build:** the Back control's hint under the control was hidden behind the bubble in the desk view (moved beside it); the builder check's first version flagged a wheel button's label (its template is sized by the layout at runtime: a parent's graphic now counts as behind its label whatever its rect) and the frame's empty labels (removed); the paper labels took two darkening steps to reach 4.5:1 as drawn under the art's tonemapping.
