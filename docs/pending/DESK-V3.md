### How the install works (DeskArt.Painted on, the default)

When the office binds and the gameplay scene holds the painted desk:
1. **Align:** the bake's frame is the game's (the art mat's centre at the origin, measured: the art mat at world z -0.52, as `layout.py` says), so the prefab sits on the bound frame, square to it, with the painted mat's pad (1.075 m in the bake, measured on the mesh) on the frame's height, where the papers lie.
2. **The map:** the painted homes take over DESK-PLAN's map by id (`DeskLayoutSettings.UsePainted`; `HomeOf`, `Bay`, the slide-in, the obstacles and DESK-PLAN's own layers all read the map in use). Each painted home is the rectangle of desk v3's layout (`layout.HOMES`, exported), as tall as its baked objects, turned as DESK-PLAN turns its gameplay object (STAMP's rack 90 degrees, the UV torch -90), its slide desk v3's (the intercom now slides right). 3. **The art's desk hides:** every renderer named by `paintedCutParts` (the desk and rails, the mat, the PC, keyboard and mouse, the phone, the till and its credits, the NEXT sign and its caption, the lamp, the old stamp, ink pad and binder, the booth partitions, the art's shadow cards, the right cork board's stability monitor) is switched off last, after every anchor was measured; their click boxes go and their readouts move to the fallback HUD.
4. **The look goes to the gameplay objects (Reskin):** the scanner, the chip reader, the stand-in intercom, the UV torch's body and DETAIN keep their click boxes and their logic, stand on their painted home's floor (+3 cm on the rail trays, +5.4 cm on DETAIN's spot on the back step) and hide their own meshes; the painted mesh rides the transform their slide or carry moves. 5. **Painted only:** the glasses slide on their own (home root, `Slide`, a click box on the Interactable layer); the bell, the pocket, the pouch, the glasses' stand, the AVAILABLE sign, the PC and the locked inserts are fixed. The PC's desktop clone draws on the painted screen quad.

### Decisions (mine)

- **The painted homes are desk v3's layout, not the meshes' boxes.** First measured on the meshes, the gate failed on boxes the designed homes do not have: the turned PC's box reached the BABEL home, the mouse's cable crossed the keyboard. UNITY_PLAN 9.3 already planned `desk_layout_v3.json` from `layout.py`, so the importer reads it; only heights come from the meshes. The mouse is the exception (measured): the bake moved it 2.5 cm left and 17 cm back with its cable, and `layout.py`'s mouse rectangle is stale. `DeskPaintedMapTests.EveryBakedObject_StandsInTheHomeDeskV3sLayoutGivesIt` ties the two (every pivot inside its home).
- **DESK-PLAN's homes desk v3 has no room for:** the art's mouse home (cut) and the later thumbprint reader (positions only; its home lies on desk v3's chip reader tray) are left out; the supply crate's spot moves 3 cm right (its click box touched the scanner tray's). The BABEL home, the Rolodex and the cards keep their WhenOwned rule; the glasses, the bell and the pocket show from day 1 (their painted object is there).
- **The painted plan's gate skips only the box sight check** (`PaintedProblems`): desk v3's sight was gated on its real render with real occlusion (the pipeline's `render/legibility_office.txt` and its slid states, all "ok"), where the boxes miss that the turned PC leaves the back step in view (they said 4% of the Rolodex). UNITY_PLAN 10.1 repeats it on Unity's ID render. Every other check passes (0 problems), and Build Office UI stops on any.
- **Only what moves casts a shadow.** The shell and the fixed objects cast none (their shadows are painted); the first capture showed a hard cast shadow across the mat from a painted fixed object.
- **The glass sheen is hidden:** `Glass_Sheen.png`'s alpha holds the painting's edges, the painted traveller's outline too (up to 40%), so it would ghost a fixed traveller over every real one. Art request: a sheen without the hall and the traveller.
- **The stability monitor and the credits move to the HUD:** the painted booth wall (x 2.30) stands in front of the art's right cork board, so its monitor is cut and the Helix River shows in the fallback HUD (top right); the till is gone from desk v3, so the credits do too. The clock and the calendar on the left board stay.
- **Unity reads the bake half a turn round:** the FBX files (forward Z, up Y) import as (-x, y, -z) of the DeskFrame with every object turned 180 degrees, not as the bake README's (x, z, y) (it was checked by a Blender round trip only). The importer turns each object back; every pivot then matches `anchors.json` within 0.01 mm. The winding and normals are right (100% of faces agree, facing the camera).

## Round 3: the review of daff612 and what changed

Each finding, its fix and the test that fails without it: every fault the review named was planted back into the code (`scratchpad/deskv3r3/mutate_r3.py`, 19 faults) and **19 of 19** turned a test red.

1. **The forced retract was undone, silent and early.** (a) `DeskSlideIn` retired and then stepped in the same frame, so a cursor still on the machine summoned it straight back; (b) `Step` cleared the hand-off cue `Retire` had raised; (c) it fired at closing time (`DeskAvailability.IsClosed`), while the last traveller may still be served (`ClosingAction.FinishCurrent`), pulling a reading chip reader home with its passport. Now the shift's end is a lasting state of the assist, `ProximityAssist.Close()`, raised by `DeskSlideIn` when the **day orchestrator's `OnDayCompleted`** fires (the last traveller finished, or the booth shut with nobody at the desk). From the next step nothing is picked or summoned, and every slider out, waiting or held retracts at once with the hand-off's reversed shush (raised inside the step, so it sounds); a busy one goes only with its docked paper riding it (`SlideCandidate.Rides`, asked of `MachineSlideIn.Rides` only then), else as soon as it is empty; one held for a drop goes once the drop hold ends (the review's minor 3: it is tried every step, not once). Tests: `AForcedRetract_TakesEverySliderHomeOnTheNextStep_WithItsSound_AndNothingSlidesAgain_WithTheCursorStillOnIt` (the cursor stays on the chip reader for 90 frames: it never comes back out, no unlatch, no shush), `AForcedRetract_TakesABusyOneOnlyWithItsPaperRiding_ElseAsSoonAsItIsEmpty_AndOneHeldForADropOnceItLanded`.
2. **Correction 2 failed for a loser caught in its anticipation**, and the plain rule left tails. `NearlySeated` now takes the distance from home either side. The cursor-only fuzz also showed that a loser just under `winnerBelow` still crawls home over several frames of its ease-in-out while the winner springs out (2 to 6 frames of both moving, depending on the frame's phase). Two rules close it: when the winner starts, a loser on its last stretch (under `winnerBelow`) takes that stretch on the next step, the one frame of the winner's first move (`Begin` sets its `SeatBy`); and a winner summoned onto a loser already on its last stretch waits one step for it to be home (a cursor darting back and forth never chains two overlaps). The stretch is at most 10% of the travel (2 px at 1080p), along the way home. Test: `ACursorWanderingTheDesk_NeverMovesTwoSlidersAtOnceForMoreThanAFrame_NorHasTwoHalfOut` (the review's fuzz, 3 x 20,000 frames, the chip reader's silhouette over the scanner's home footprint as on the desk: 258 frames with two sliders moving more than 0.25 px, **every one a single frame**, never two half out), and `HandOff_FromALoserCaughtBehindHome...` (the review's harness: caught 1 and 2 frames in, the winner starts once the loser is within 10% of home, the loser is home on the winner's first move, no step home larger than its last stretch).
3. **The slide-in's key sounds were silent** (`machine_slide_unlatch`, `machine_slide_stop`, `machine_seat` are not in `SoundBank_Default`, `drawer_close` is no bank cue, and lifts played nothing). Every cue, for every slider, now plays its own id when the bank has it and else its stand-in through the house fallback, `CueSounds` (the stand-in's bank clip, else its placeholder), on the desk bus: the bank's `paper_slide` for the shushes (and the hand-off's), the relay clack low (pitch 0.6) for the unlatch tick, the cartridge thunk low (0.7) for the knock, the cover snap for the seat's clack (`DeskSlides.Sound`, its stand-in knobs). Test: `DeskSlidesTests.EveryCue_SoundsForEverySlider_ItsStandInABankClipOrAPlaceholder_UntilTheSoundListDeliversIt` (reads the bank asset and `CueSounds.cs`: each stand-in has a clip or a placeholder).

### Logic: `ProximityAssist`, `SlideCurve`, `SlideOccupancy` (Visuals, pure; DESK-HOMES)
- **Nearest wins, one at a time** (`Pick`): the front-most silhouette under the cursor wins at once, whatever is out (a busy one too, keeping every other home); else a home footprint under the cursor's desk point (the one out keeps it); else the nearest within the trigger, the one out keeping it unless another is nearer by more than the hysteresis. `Rank` ranks the silhouettes the ray hits, each slider once.
- **Hand-off:** the loser retracts at once (ease-in-out, no linger, no seat; the reversed shush); the winner waits until the loser is nearly seated (within 10% of home, either side), never longer than `winnerAfter`, and the loser's last stretch takes the winner's first frame; a winner summoned onto a loser already on its last stretch waits for it to be home. **Decision (mine):** a slider still going home when the cursor reaches the next machine gives way as a hand-off loser too: hurried home within `winnerAfter` (only ever faster, arriving at speed, no clack, no seat bounce), or, already in its seat bounce, resting first; so one machine moves at a time.
- **Out:** the anticipation (a track backs away from the mat; a lift never dips, it squashes), then its own spring with one overshoot, settled within max(2%, 0.5 px) from 250 ms; from wherever it stood. **Back:** after the linger, the ease-in home and the seat bounce on the arrival frame, with the clack.
- **Busy or occupied stays as it is:** out, it stays out (it never lingers home; one lingering that turns busy goes back out); on its way, it stops where it stands (`Held`) until empty; at home it is not summoned; it never loses a hand-off; another empty machine may slide meanwhile. **Decision (mine): the drop hold.** A machine a paper was let go on stays out as if busy for 1 s while the paper lands, until the machine's own busy state takes over.
- **In hand** (the UV torch picked up): it gives way at once as a hand-off loser and is not summoned while held. **Gone from the desk:** home at once.
- **The shift is over** (`Close`): item 1 above.
- **Reduced Motion or Motion intensity 0:** every move is a one-frame cut (no anticipation, overshoot, seat, squash or sway); the travel never scales; the cues still sound. **Decision (mine):** UNITY_PLAN 5.2's "a 120 ms cut both ways" is read as G2's cut (DESK-HOMES P5: "a one-frame cut").
- **Felt gap:** a track never goes past its felt gap (`DeskSlider.Reach`).
- **Cues** (`SlideCueKind`): the unlatch on the trigger frame, the shush as the base moves (40 ms), the knock at the peak, the reversed shush going home, the clack on the arrival frame of a way home of its own, the hand-off's reversed shush.
- **Pose** (`SlidePose`): the position, a lift's squash, the sway, the contact shadow (alpha, blur, widening). **Where it puts it** (`SlideCurve.Offset`, `SquashScale`, `ShadowCentre`, `ShadowScale`).
- **`SlideOccupancy`:** the scanner's occupancy from its outside, and the rider (`Rider`).
- **Decision (mine):** all of it lives in Visuals, not Domain as DESK-HOMES' spec proposed, because the slide data is checked against the home map (`DeskHome`, Visuals) and Visuals cannot see Domain.
