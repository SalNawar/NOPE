### Logic: `ProximityAssist`, `SlideCurve`, `SlideOccupancy` (Visuals, pure; DESK-HOMES)
- **Nearest wins, one at a time** (`Pick`): the front-most silhouette under the cursor wins at once, whatever is out (a busy one too, keeping every other home); else a home footprint under the cursor's desk point (the one out keeps it); else the nearest within the trigger, the one out keeping it unless another is nearer by more than the hysteresis. `Rank` ranks the silhouettes the ray hits, each slider once.
- **Hand-off:** the loser retracts at once (ease-in-out, no linger, no seat; the reversed shush); the winner waits until the loser is nearly seated (within 10% of home, either side), never longer than `winnerAfter`, and the loser's last stretch takes the winner's first frame; a winner summoned onto a loser already on its last stretch waits for it to be home. **Decision (mine):** a slider still going home when the cursor reaches the next machine gives way as a hand-off loser too: hurried home within `winnerAfter` (only ever faster, arriving at speed, no clack, no seat bounce), or, already in its seat bounce, resting first; so one machine moves at a time.
- **Out:** the anticipation (a track backs away from the mat; a lift never dips, it squashes), then its own spring with one overshoot, settled within max(2%, 0.5 px) from 250 ms; from wherever it stood. **Back:** after the linger, the ease-in home and the seat bounce on the arrival frame, with the clack.
- **Busy or occupied stays as it is:** out, it stays out (it never lingers home; one lingering that turns busy goes back out); on its way, it stops where it stands (`Held`) until empty; at home it is not summoned; it never loses a hand-off; another empty machine may slide meanwhile. **Decision (mine): the drop hold.** A machine a paper was let go on stays out as if busy for 1 s while the paper lands, until the machine's own busy state takes over.
- **In hand** (the UV torch picked up): it gives way at once as a hand-off loser and is not summoned while held. **Gone from the desk:** home at once.
- **The shift is over** (`Close`): item 1 above.
- **Reduced Motion or Motion intensity 0:** every move is a one-frame cut (no anticipation, overshoot, seat, squash or sway); the travel never scales; the cues still sound. **Decision (mine):** UNITY_PLAN 5.2's "a 120 ms cut both ways" is read as G2's cut (DESK-HOMES P5: "a one-frame cut").
- **Felt gap:** a track never goes past its felt gap (`DeskSlider.Reach`).
- **Cues** (`SlideCueKind`): the unlatch on the trigger frame, the shush as the base moves (40 ms), the knock at the peak, the reversed shush going home, the clack on the arrival frame of a way home of its own, the hand-off's reversed shush.
- **Pose** (`SlidePose`): the position, a lift's squash, the sway, the contact shadow (alpha, blur, widening). **Where it puts it** (`SlideCurve.Offset`, `SquashScale`, `ShadowCentre`, `ShadowScale`).
- **`SlideOccupancy`:** the scanner's occupancy from its outside, and the rider (`Rider`).
- **Decision (mine):** all of it lives in Visuals, not Domain as DESK-HOMES' spec proposed, because the slide data is checked against the home map (`DeskHome`, Visuals) and Visuals cannot see Domain.
