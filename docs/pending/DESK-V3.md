## What was built

### Data: `DeskSlides` (Visuals, pure; DESK-HOMES)
`Assets/Scripts/Visuals/DeskSlides.cs`. The whole of `render/slides.json` as the existing desk config's own section: `DeskLayoutSettings.slides` (a section of `DeskConfigSO`, `Desk_Default.asset`; the asset holds no layout section yet, so the approved defaults load without an asset edit).
- **Knobs:** the trigger (120 px around the silhouette at 1080p, 40 px hysteresis, scaled by the screen height: `ToReference`), out (250 ms, 40 ms anticipation), back (150 ms linger, 300 ms ease-in, 90 ms seat), the hand-off (130 ms retract, winner below 0.1 or after 130 ms), the drop hold (1 s), the scanner's lid read (0.5 degrees), the felt gap (0.4 cm), the lift squash (1.5 px, 60 ms release), the lift and track shadows and the peak's press (2 frames), the five sound ids and their stand-ins (`Sound`).
- **Sliders** (`DeskSlider`): the scanner 4.1 cm and the chip reader 3.7 cm along their 6 cm tracks; the intercom 2.5 cm (its mic sways 4 px at 7 Hz) and the BABEL tuner 3.5 cm; the UV torch, the glasses (7 degree tip toward the chair), the Rolodex, the protocol cards, both daters and the rulebook rise in place; DETAIN, AVAILABLE, the bell and the deal tray never move. Each has its own spring as the gate solved it and, for a track, its painted cut and the felt it shows at rest. A track's way is its home's.
- **Rules:** `Problems(homes, reach)` (the builder's plan gate runs it: `DeskLayoutSettings.Problems`): an id twice; a slider with no home; a track beyond the 6 cm reach, past its felt gap or out of its painted track at the peak; a home declaring a slide no track of it slides; a spring that does not overshoot once or whose damping disagrees with its overshoot; a winner that may wait longer than the retract. `Obstacle` is a home's swept box.

### Logic: `ProximityAssist`, `SlideCurve`, `SlideOccupancy` (Visuals, pure; DESK-HOMES)
- **Nearest wins, one at a time** (`Pick`): the front-most silhouette under the cursor wins at once, whatever is out (a busy one too, keeping every other home); else a home footprint under the cursor's desk point (the one out keeps it); else the nearest within the trigger, the one out keeping it unless another is nearer by more than the hysteresis. `Rank` ranks the silhouettes the ray hits, each slider once.
- **Hand-off:** the loser retracts at once (ease-in-out, no linger, no seat; the reversed shush); the winner waits until the loser is nearly seated (within 10% of home, either side), never longer than `winnerAfter`, and the loser's last stretch takes the winner's first frame; a winner summoned onto a loser already on its last stretch waits for it to be home. **Decision (mine):** a slider still going home when the cursor reaches the next machine gives way as a hand-off loser too: hurried home within `winnerAfter` (only ever faster, arriving at speed, no clack, no seat bounce), or, already in its seat bounce, resting first; so one machine moves at a time.
- **Out:** the anticipation (a track backs away from the mat; a lift never dips, it squashes), then its own spring with one overshoot, settled within max(2%, 0.5 px) from 250 ms; from wherever it stood. **Back:** after the linger, the ease-in home and the seat bounce on the arrival frame, with the clack.
- **Busy or occupied stays as it is:** out, it stays out (it never lingers home; one lingering that turns busy goes back out); on its way, it stops where it stands (`Held`) until empty; at home it is not summoned; it never loses a hand-off; another empty machine may slide meanwhile. **Decision (mine): the drop hold.** A machine a paper was let go on stays out as if busy for 1 s while the paper lands, until the machine's own busy state takes over.
- **In hand** (the UV torch picked up): it gives way at once as a hand-off loser and is not summoned while held. **Gone from the desk:** home at once.
- **The shift is over** (`Close`): item 1 above.
- **Reduced Motion or Motion intensity 0:** every move is a one-frame cut (no anticipation, overshoot, seat, squash or sway); the travel never scales; the cues still sound. **Decision (mine):** UNITY_PLAN 5.2's "a 120 ms cut both ways" is read as G2's cut (DESK-HOMES P5: "a one-frame cut").
- **Felt gap:** a track never goes past its felt gap (`DeskSlider.Reach`).
- **Cues** (`SlideCueKind`): the unlatch on the trigger frame, the shush as the base moves (40 ms), the knock at the peak, the reversed shush going home, the clack on the arrival frame of a way home of its own, the hand-off's reversed shush.
- **Pose** (`SlidePose`): the position, a lift's squash, the sway, the contact shadow (alpha, blur, widening). **Where it puts it** (`SlideCurve.Offset`, `SquashScale`, `ShadowCentre`, `ShadowScale`).
- **`SlideOccupancy`:** the scanner's occupancy from its outside, and the rider (`Rider`).
- **Decision (mine):** all of it lives in Visuals, not Domain as DESK-HOMES' spec proposed, because the slide data is checked against the home map (`DeskHome`, Visuals) and Visuals cannot see Domain.

### Unity: `DeskSlideIn`, `MachineSlideIn`, `SlideShadow` (DESK-HOMES)
- `DeskSlideIn`: one per desk. Each frame it gathers every attached slider's facts on the office camera (`SlideCandidate.Found`), steps the assist, sounds the cues (`DeskSlides.Sound`: the bank's clip, else the stand-in through `CueSounds` on its own voice on the desk bus) and gives each slider its pose; it closes the assist when the day orchestrator's `OnDayCompleted` fires. While the PC is open (or `InputGate.Blocked`) nothing is near. Allocation-free per frame.
- `MachineSlideIn`: one per slider; `MachineSlideIn.Attach(id, root, ...)` is the hook every owner binds through (UNITY_PLAN 5.3: BABEL-TUNER, GLASSES). It writes only its slide transform: the object's `Slide` child when it has one, else the object itself; its rest is where it stands when attached, measured once its scale stands still at home. A track moves along its home's way in `DeskLayout.Frame`, a lift rises; a lift squashes and tips; a "Mic" under the slide sways; its contact shadow (`SlideShadow`); a riding paper in a forced retract. For an object whose owner poses it every frame (the UV torch's body), the offset is a layer put on after the owner and taken off before it.
- **Bound by the office binder** (`OfficeSceneBinder.BindSlideIn`, after the desk is laid): item 8's list, the day orchestrator (`FindAnyObjectByType<DayOrchestrator>`), and the papers' shadow material (the rulebook's `Shadow` part).
- **Obstacles:** `DeskLayout.RefreshObstacles` registers each home swept by what rests in it (`DeskSlides.Obstacle`) in `DeskLayout.Obstacles`, which `DeskDecor` reads; the carry does not (item 9).

### The D6 framing focus: `docs/pending/DESK-V3.focus.patch` (DESK-HOMES; applied after CAM's merge)
`SlideFocus` (pure) picks the target: the cursor's machine once its slide has finished plus a 150 ms dwell; it re-aims to the next machine without letting go; it lets go when the cursor leaves or the picked machine will not come; at most one start per 0.8 s (BUG-17); `Allowed`: the reading view only (DESK-HOMES Q5's default), nothing held (`HoldRegistry` or a dragged paper), no Cinemachine blend (the drop tilt), motion not still; it scales with the Motion intensity. `FramingFocus` (`Assets/Scripts/Office/Desk/FramingFocus.cs`, target only) hands it to **`CameraFeel.Focus`**, the one lens writer: from the rest lens `DeskView.ReadingFov`, 3% of the lens the view entered at, push-in and focus together never past 8% (`SlideFocus.Narrowing`, `Total`), a turn of at most 3% of the frame through a `CinemachineRecomposer` on the reading camera (applied after its body, so the impulses still add on top), on the push-in's spring, each move tagged `SlideInFocus`; refused out of the reading view and when motion is still (a cut, tagged). **Decision (mine):** a hold or a tilt that begins while a machine is framed lets the focus ease out on that spring rather than cut; none starts while either lasts. **To land it:** once this branch is rebased on a main that holds CAM, run `git show HEAD:docs/pending/DESK-V3.focus.patch | git apply --3way` (the committed blob has LF line ends), delete the patch, run every check, and commit as DESK-HOMES (CAM's lease of `CameraFeel.cs` has passed by then). If CAM's `CameraFeel.cs` changed after fc50757, `--3way` merges small drifts; otherwise redo its hunk by hand from the patch. The slide-in does not ship without it (DESK-HOMES section 8): the branch merges only after Phase A, so the patch lands in the same integration.
