## What was built

### Data: `DeskSlides` (Visuals, pure; DESK-HOMES)
`Assets/Scripts/Visuals/DeskSlides.cs`. The whole of `render/slides.json` as the existing desk config's own section: `DeskLayoutSettings.slides` (a section of `DeskConfigSO`, `Desk_Default.asset`; the asset holds no layout section yet, so the approved defaults load without an asset edit).
- **Knobs:** the trigger (120 px around the silhouette at 1080p, 40 px hysteresis, scaled by the screen height), out (250 ms, 40 ms anticipation), back (150 ms linger, 300 ms ease-in, 90 ms seat), the hand-off (130 ms retract, winner below 0.1 or after 130 ms), the drop hold (1 s), the scanner's lid read (0.5 degrees), the felt gap (0.4 cm and 3 px), the lamp gap (3 px), the lift squash (1.5 px, 60 ms release), the lift and track shadows and the peak's press, the five sound ids, and the D6 focus (150 ms dwell, one start per 0.8 s, 3% narrowing, 8% cap with the push-in, 3% aim).
- **Sliders** (`DeskSlider`): the scanner 4.1 cm and the chip reader 3.7 cm along their 6 cm tracks; the intercom 2.5 cm (its mic sways 4 px at 7 Hz) and the BABEL tuner 3.5 cm; the UV torch, the glasses (7 degree tip toward the chair), the Rolodex, the protocol cards, both daters and the rulebook rise in place; DETAIN, AVAILABLE, the bell and the deal tray never move. Each has its own spring as the gate solved it and, for a track, its painted cut and the felt it shows at rest. A track's way is its home's.
- **Rules:** `Problems(homes, reach)` (the builder's plan gate runs it: `DeskLayoutSettings.Problems`): an id twice; a slider with no home; a track beyond the 6 cm reach, past its felt gap or out of its painted track at the peak; a home declaring a slide no track of it slides; a spring that does not overshoot once or whose damping disagrees with its overshoot; a winner that may wait longer than the retract; the focus's budgets. `FeltHolds` and `LampHolds` are the gate's clearance rules on a measure. `Obstacle` is a home's swept box.

### Logic: `ProximityAssist`, `SlideCurve`, `SlideFocus`, `SlideOccupancy` (Visuals, pure; DESK-HOMES)
- **Nearest wins, one at a time** (`Pick`): the front-most silhouette under the cursor wins at once, whatever is out (a busy one too, keeping every other home); else a home footprint under the cursor's desk point (the one out keeps it); else the nearest within the trigger, the one out keeping it unless another is nearer by more than the hysteresis.
- **Hand-off:** the loser retracts at once (ease-in-out, no linger, no seat; the reversed shush); the winner waits until the loser is nearly seated, never longer than `winnerAfter`. **Decision (mine):** a slider still going home when the cursor reaches the next machine gives way as a hand-off loser too: hurried home within `winnerAfter` (only ever faster, arriving at speed with its clack, no seat bounce), or, already in its seat bounce, resting first; so one machine moves at a time.
- **Out:** the anticipation (a track backs away from the mat; a lift never dips, it squashes), then its own spring with one overshoot, settled within max(2%, 0.5 px) from 250 ms; from wherever it stood. **Back:** after the linger, the ease-in home and the seat bounce on the arrival frame.
- **Busy or occupied stays as it is:** out, it stays out (it never lingers home); on its way, it stops where it stands (`Held`) until empty; at home it is not summoned; it never loses a hand-off; another empty machine may slide meanwhile. **Decision (mine): the drop hold.** A machine a paper was let go on stays out as if busy for `dropHoldSeconds` (1 s) while the paper lands, until the machine's own busy state takes over (the chip reader's read starts only once the paper is on its pad).
- **In hand** (the UV torch picked up): it gives way at once as a hand-off loser and is not summoned while held. **Gone from the desk:** home at once.
- **Forced retract** (`Retire`, closing time): above.
- **Reduced Motion or Motion intensity 0:** every move is a one-frame cut (no anticipation, overshoot, seat, squash or sway); the travel never scales; the cues still sound. **Decision (mine):** UNITY_PLAN 5.2's "a 120 ms cut both ways" is read as G2's cut (DESK-HOMES P5: "a one-frame cut").
- **Felt gap:** a track never goes past its felt gap (`DeskSlider.Reach`).
- **Cues** (`SlideCueKind`): the unlatch on the trigger frame, the shush as the base moves (40 ms), the knock at the peak, the reversed shush going home, the clack on the arrival frame (a hurried way home's too), the hand-off.
- **Pose** (`SlidePose`): the position, a lift's squash, the sway, the contact shadow (alpha, blur, widening).
- **`SlideFocus`:** the D6 target (below). **`SlideOccupancy`:** the scanner's occupancy from its outside (above).
- **Decision (mine):** all of it lives in Visuals, not Domain as DESK-HOMES' spec proposed, because the slide data is checked against the home map (`DeskHome`, Visuals) and Visuals cannot see Domain.

### Unity: `DeskSlideIn`, `MachineSlideIn`, `SlideShadow`, `FramingFocus` (DESK-HOMES)
- `DeskSlideIn`: one per desk. Each frame it gathers every attached slider's facts on the office camera, steps the assist, sounds the cues (a machine without its clip yet plays `paper_slide` for the shushes and `drawer_close` for the seat; a lift plays only its own clips) and gives each slider its pose; the forced retract on the desk's closing. While the PC is open (or `InputGate.Blocked`) nothing is near. Allocation-free per frame.
- `MachineSlideIn`: one per slider; `MachineSlideIn.Attach(id, root, ...)` is the hook every owner binds through (UNITY_PLAN 5.3: BABEL-TUNER, GLASSES). It writes only its slide transform: the object's `Slide` child when it has one, else the object itself; its rest is where it stands when attached. A track moves along its home's way in `DeskLayout.Frame`, a lift rises; a lift squashes and tips; a "Mic" under the slide sways; its contact shadow (`SlideShadow`); a riding paper in a forced retract. For an object whose owner poses it every frame (the UV torch's body), the offset is a layer put on after the owner and taken off before it.
- `FramingFocus` (`Assets/Scripts/Office/Desk/FramingFocus.cs`, UNITY_PLAN 5.3): **the D6 framing focus**. `SlideFocus` (pure) picks the target: the cursor's machine once its slide has finished plus a 150 ms dwell; it re-aims to the next machine without letting go; it lets go when the cursor leaves or the picked machine will not come; at most one start per 0.8 s (BUG-17). Off unless the reading view is on (DESK-HOMES Q5's default, the reading view only), nothing is held (`HoldRegistry` or a dragged paper), no Cinemachine blend runs (the drop tilt) and motion is not still; it scales with the Motion intensity. `FramingFocus` hands the target to **`CameraFeel.Focus`**, the one lens writer: the reading view narrows by 3% of its bound field of view on top of the 2.5 degree push-in, the two never past 8% (46 degrees at A1; `SlideFocus.Narrowing`), and turns at most 3% of the frame toward the machine through a `CinemachineRecomposer` on the desk camera (pan and tilt from `SlideFocus.Turn`, applied after the camera's body so the impulses still add on top; set up as its Reset would, a runtime add skipping Reset), all on the push-in's Heavy spring; a snap under Reduced Motion. **Decision (mine):** a hold or a tilt that begins while a machine is framed lets the focus ease out on that spring rather than cut (a cut would pop the lens by up to 1.5 degrees); none starts while either lasts.
- **Bound by the office binder** (`OfficeSceneBinder.BindSlideIn`, after the desk is laid): the gameplay scanner when it stands on its home (an art office's own scanner stays put: its meshes are the art's), busy while `SlideOccupancy` says so, its dock the scanner; the chip reader, busy while it reads (`ChipReader.Busy`), its dock the reader; the intercom when it is the built stand-in and its home declares a slide (not on today's map); the BABEL rack, the Rolodex and the protocol cards; the UV torch's body as a layer that gives way when the torch is picked up (`UvTorch.Held`); then the focus.
- **Obstacles:** `DeskLayout.RefreshObstacles` registers each home swept by what rests in it (`DeskSlides.Obstacle`), so a carried paper never needs a live read.
