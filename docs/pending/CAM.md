# CAM (Phase A #6): the camera stays put and frames the work at every aspect

Branch `fix/a-cam`, cut from `fix/a-desk-plan` (DESK-PLAN #5, because CAM frames the new desk plan) and rebased onto `origin/main` (RUN-SAFE folded in). Sources: FIX_PLAN.md §3 CAM, FIX_PLAN_v2.md §3 "CAM: delta" (v2 wins), BUGLIST.md, DECISIONS_1019a.md, GAME_FEEL_BIBLE.md (CAM's rows: H1 and every CS cell; G2, G9, L1), PHASEB/LEASES.md, docs/pending/CARVE.md, CUTS.md, QA-RIG.md, DESK-PLAN.md (its CAM hand-off).

**What it is:** two fixed camera poses per aspect (the office pose and the tilted reading pose a desk drop goes to), solved so the big mat, the tools beside it and the deal tray are always in frame and the floor under the desk never shows; every camera move tagged with its cause; no view change while something is held; the controls of a view appear only over the last third of the move into it; the shake table; the city view without bars and with a parallax that never lurches. Code only: no Unity was run (another agent holds the Unity lane), so every in-engine check is a recipe in RESULT below.

## Decisions that are ours (proposals; each a data or knob change)

1. **"In frame" = the inner 8 cm strip of each side tool, not the whole tool.** v1's 7b pass line said "every in-use tool fully inside the reading frame". With the big mat (1.46 m) and the rail beside it (to x 1.25), the whole rail in frame would show papers at about 45%. DESK-PLAN's hand-off asked for ≥ 8 x 8 cm of each; the machines slide in 6 cm toward the mat when the cursor nears them (D2, DESK-HOMES). Result: papers at 62% at 16:9 (Saleh's "~67%"). Knob: `DeskViewTuning.sideReach`.
2. **The margins** (top 8% of the half screen for the case HUD's strip and the Back control, 3% bottom, 4% sides). The pull tabs are hidden in the reading view, so the side margin does not need to clear them (clearing them would cost papers 62% → 57%).
3. **The reading push-in stays** (Bible L1: "the push-in stays") but ends on the solved pose: the lens starts `readingPushIn` (2.5°) wider and settles to the solved 50°, so the framing is exact at rest.
4. **The pull tabs hide in the reading view** (PC and STAMPS). Q, the PC itself and TAB still work. STAMP decides the STAMPS tab's future (D4).
5. **The city crops instead of letterboxing** (BUG-42 over the 2026-10-07 "see the FULL picture"): about 59% of the painting's width shows at 16:9. CITY-LIFE's new backdrop (greybox v7f) can be painted for the screen's shape.
6. **Shake rows picked by the callers' knobs**, so FeelDirector and UiPrint stay untouched; the knobs' sizes and lengths now only pick a row. FEEDBACK can later call per moment.
7. **The office pose's art width** (`LensFit.ArtAspect` 2.1) is a constant next to `DesignAspect`: a fact of the hall art, measured from the audit's capture.
