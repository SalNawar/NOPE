## The Thinker

- Original sculpture: Auguste Rodin, *The Thinker*.
- Digital source: **Scan the World / Jonathan Beck**, 18 August 2014.
- [Source and license declaration](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Scan_the_World_-_The_Thinker_(Auguste_Rodin).stl).
- License: [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- Adaptation: normalized scale/orientation, scanner-noise smoothing, approximately 7,000 triangles and simplified bronze/patina regions; no scanned texture.
- The derived `Art_Thinker.fbx`, its prefab and the `Art_Thinker` collection in `Sculptures.blend` are also provided under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Release credit: “The Thinker — digital source by Scan the World / Jonathan Beck, CC BY-SA 4.0; adapted for Time Sorter with reduced geometry and stylized materials.” Include source/license links above. No endorsement is implied.

## Discobolus

- Sculpture after Myron; replica in Den Kongelige Afstøbningssamling, Copenhagen.
- Digitization: **Statens Museum for Kunst**.
- [Source and CC0 declaration](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Diskoskasteren_(Discobolos)_-_KAS1549.stl).
- [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

## Nefertiti

- Historic subject: bust of Nefertiti. Digital source: **Scan the World**, 14 October 2014.
- [Source and CC0 declaration](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bust_of_Nefertiti_at_the_Neues_Museum,_Berlin.stl).
- [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- Adapted through orientation/scale normalization, smoothing, approximately 7,000 triangles and a limited limestone/crown/band palette.
- This is the Scan the World file linked above, not the separately published museum FOIA scan.

## Other assets

Desk hardware, hall architecture, portal ring, frames, furniture, vehicles and megablocks are authored for this project. Historical machines reuse the project's earlier authored geometry with welded surfaces, revised normals, edge treatments, a new palette and additional fittings. They are interpretations, not museum scans.

Canvas textures and the Voyager diagram reuse the project's generated images. UV mapping excludes earlier painted frames/backgrounds. Historical identity references are in `../EXHIBIT_INVENTORY.md`. The PC uses the user's ReStory screenshot as reference; its model is unbranded. Tabletop albedo provenance is in `../BlenderPC/README.md`.
