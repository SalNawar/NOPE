ONE production game background painting, very wide 3:1 canvas approximately 2172x724.
CRITICAL VIEWPOINT: the viewer is on an extremely HIGH FLOOR in the tallest administrative megatower, ABOVE the roofline of almost all surrounding buildings, looking DIAGONALLY DOWN about 25-30 degrees across a huge city. Most of the frame consists of CLEARLY VISIBLE BROAD ROOFTOP TOP PLANES with their vertical facades descending into deep urban canyons below. The tops/crowns of all major featured buildings must be WITHIN THE FRAME and visible from ABOVE: NO tall foreground facades cropped by the top edge, NO eye-level street skyline, NO close street alleys or pedestrian-scale viewpoint. No drone camera hardware in image; this is the outside VIEW through a very high occupied-floor window. Distant horizon/sky is a narrow band along the top 15%, the city fills 85%; the foreground is neighboring high-rise roofs well below the viewer, midground is a sea of tremendous arcologies whose roofs can be seen, foreground architecture is not beside our feet. Consistent three-dimensional perspective: large visible roof top planes are the dominant depth cue. The enormous vertical height reads in exposed facades and haze far below.

DESIGN MUST READ CYBERPUNK, not a normal twentieth-century city with ornamental towers. Bold dense retro future megastructures and social inequality. Hand-painted late-1980s anime environmental art, meticulous ink contours, painted cel shadows, dirty tactile surfaces and credible mechanical detail. Vibrant turquoise/cobalt, coral-magenta signage, orange-rust, jade and cream/gold. Warm golden MORNING sun from upper-left; saturated cool teal shadows, slight smog deep below. No night scene and no photorealistic 3D.

CLEAR DISTINCT GEOGRAPHIC DISTRICTS, composition:
LEFT 40%: a dense WORKING-CLASS high-rise district of bulky clustered orange and teal residential megablocks, 35-70-storey structures with enormous flat roofs split into patched communal service terraces, water tanks, cooling equipment, rooftop shanty additions at tiny realistic scale, crowded stacked balconies, exposed cabling, corroded pipes and many bright vertical cyan/pink/red analog commercial sign panels. Shapes varied, asymmetric and improvised. Dirty, worn, inhabited. Left district has several enormous readable 100-floor apartment arcologies with distinctive broad roof shapes, not ordinary little buildings.
CENTER 35%: wealthy CORPORATE/CIVIC sector of huge physically plausible Art Deco cyberpunk arcologies, much taller and bulkier than normal office towers, stepped broad jade/black/copper monoliths, wide octagonal mechanical crowns seen from ABOVE, monumental geometric buttresses, glowing facade bands, external service/elevator rails. Precisely designed wealthy building clusters and plaza roofs contrast sharply with the dense poorer blocks. A massive HUMAN bronze worker statue on one high civic podium/terrace, seen from elevated angle and smaller than surrounding megastructures. Distinct landmark: a massive multi-tiered crowned financial arcology with inset magenta/teal illuminated corporate graphics. Roofs/crowns fully visible and NOT cut off at top edge.
RIGHT 25%: INDUSTRIAL high-rise district of giant stained charcoal/rust refinery and freight superblocks, broad mechanical roofs, dense arrays of cylindrical tanks, ducts, chimney groups, cable masts and freight crane yards far below. Heavier horizontal mechanical forms contrasting corporate polished geometry and crowded residential slabs. Sea/port can be a thin far-distance detail.

Tower magnitude through scale references: tiny rooftop access doors and equipment, many floors down the faces, miniature far-below traffic streets barely seen; twenty-plus immense varied high-rise/megablock forms across depth. The city feels like a vast inhabited machine, strongly cyberpunk but HUMAN, grounded retro near future. Grit, soot, patches, worn signs, different generations of construction and pronounced social contrast. No uniform repeating Manhattan/Empire State skyline, no normal European civic dome city.
INFRASTRUCTURE RULE: NO bridges, skybridges, elevated rails spanning arbitrary tower floors, floating walkways or enormous horizontal unexplained beams. Road infrastructure stays far below in canyons; building service gantries must attach directly to their own buildings. Do not add any free-floating structures. No aliens, monsters, fantasy, mechs, spacecraft, flying islands or pristine utopia.
Only exterior city. No foreground window frame, hall, railing, desk or observer. No painted flying cars/aircraft, they will be animated separately. No text labels, watermark or multi-panel layout.
This is a new composition, not a variation of an old street-level skyline. Visible roofs, high view, clearly different districts, obvious retro-cyberpunk technology and coherent structural logic are mandatory.
