# Archived exhibit inventory

All thirteen historical subjects, their plinths, gallery rails and suspension wires were removed from `Assets/Scenes/OfficeScene.unity` at the user's request after the three art passes. The assets and credits remain available. The inventory below records their former placement; none are currently installed.
The printing press, Wright Flyer, Rocket, three sculptures and record cover are 3D
meshes. The paintings have 3D frames with separately mapped canvas images. Wear is
fictional. The central portal approach stays open, with displays, plinths, a short
locomotive base and ceiling suspension wires. Sculpture source credits and licenses
are in `BlenderOffice/SOURCES.md`.

The floor form is functional discarded paperwork rather than memorabilia. Hall benches, devices and portal hardware are functional furnishings. The sky, buildings and traffic are environmental assets. No generic helmet, globe, wheel, compass, pocket watch or decorative vase was added to this pack.

## Booth papers and keepsake: iteration 32

The Voyager cover is the single named desk keepsake. The duplicate hall cover and all duplicate postcards/miniature sculptures were removed with the superseded booth shelves. There is one installed instance of each of the thirteen named subjects above.

Pinned administrative slips, form sorter, clipped paperwork, pencils, floppy disks and sleeve are functional office supplies. Labels remain blank for history/language changes. The newspaper stays folded at the side. Picture rails provide mounting support. Further hall composition changes are deferred at the user's request.
