# Named exhibit inventory

These thirteen named exhibits are installed in `Assets/Scenes/OfficeScene.unity`.
The printing press, Wright Flyer, Rocket, three sculptures and record cover are 3D
meshes. The paintings have 3D frames with separately mapped canvas images. Wear is
fictional. The central portal approach stays open, with displays, plinths, a short
locomotive base and ceiling suspension wires. Sculpture source credits and licenses
are in `BlenderOffice/SOURCES.md`.

| Asset | Installed location | Narrative use | Authored treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mona Lisa | Left display rail | An immensely valued object treated as routine office decoration | Simplified framed painting with wear |
| Girl with a Pearl Earring | Right display rail | Art preservation versus careless bureaucracy | Simplified framed painting with wear |
| The Starry Night | Left rear display | A recovered cultural landmark outliving its caretakers | Simplified damaged frame and image |
| The Great Wave | Right rear display stand | A cultural landmark reduced to worn office decor | Worn print and tilted frame |
| The Milkmaid | Leaning near the left middle display | Everyday life preserved across eras | Graphic shapes and damaged frame |
| Composition after Mondrian | Fallen near the right middle display | A bureaucracy cataloging abstract art without understanding it | Original geometric interpretation with broken frame corner |
| The Thinker | Left front plinth | The visual joke of thought and deliberation in a paperwork office | Flat bronze planes, dust patches, chipped base |
| Discobolus | Right front plinth | Human achievement left waiting under government care | Flat stone planes and a damaged discus edge |
| Nefertiti bust | Left middle plinth | A historic portrait reduced to a poorly maintained civic exhibit | Simplified bust, chipped shoulder, plain crown bands |
| 1903 Wright Flyer | Suspended above the right middle hall | Aviation as a possible technology milestone | 3D display model with front canard, twin rear propellers, skids and worn muslin surfaces |
| Gutenberg-style screw press reconstruction | Right middle floor display | Dissemination of knowledge and political ideas | 3D timber frame, helical screw, lever, iron fittings and a blank sheet |
| Stephenson's Rocket | Left rear scale-model display | Transport and industrialization | 3D 1829-pattern interpretation with two axles, larger driving wheels, inclined cylinders and tall chimney |
| Voyager Golden Record cover | Small plinth left of the central approach; a keepsake also lies safely on the desk | A message to the future becoming a bureaucrat's keepsake | Simplified diagrams, worn edge and a physical backing support |

The floor form is functional discarded paperwork rather than memorabilia. Hall benches, devices and portal hardware are functional furnishings. The sky, buildings and traffic are environmental assets. No generic helmet, globe, wheel, compass, pocket watch or decorative vase was added to this pack.
