# Woman base — raw candidate

Selected second generation from the original figure guide and office palette reference, using the built-in image tool. Opaque green background; full 1024 × 1536 canvas. Measured visible figure bounds: x290–732, y239–1500. The full adult figure, empty hands and bare feet are present. The first generation was rejected because its background was dark/transparent rather than opaque green.

The scalp is approximately 21 pixels above the guide landmark. Exact registration, skin-swatch matching and extraction remain downstream work. The working magenta mannequin is a fitting reference, not a certified game-ready base. No character asset has been imported into the game.
