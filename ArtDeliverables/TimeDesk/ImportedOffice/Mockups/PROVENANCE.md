## Prompts / design constraints

First pass: one widescreen first-person time-immigration government office;
recognisable imported asset shapes; retro CRT left tilted inward, mouse to its
right; walnut desk and ivory/petrol sci-fi modules, rotary phone, calculator,
banker lamp, document tray, stamp and restrained stationery; empty central mat.
Clear middle ground, three windowed walls, visible coffered ceiling and strip
lights, four blank banners, blank departures board, centred industrial portal,
grounded enormous futuristic city. ReStory's restrained painted surfaces and
soft shaded forms; only NEXT readable; other changing screens and text blank.

Second pass: retain the first composition and shapes, remove the extra vertical
computer tower, make floor matte, reduce realistic grain and reflections, soften
material highlights, retain dimensional bevels and contact shadows. Paint the
megacity as simpler layered 2D art. Hybrid 3D hardware/architecture with flat
paper/notice sprites, separately animated cars and a portal effect. No added
middle-ground furniture, historical clutter, cups, hearts or moon ornaments.
