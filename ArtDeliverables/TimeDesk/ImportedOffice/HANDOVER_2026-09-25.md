## Confirmed diagnosis of the floor lines

The previous assistant initially suspected shadow acne or modular gaps. Tests narrowed this down:

- `42_surface_baseline`: reproduced the user's dashed lines and long dark streaks.
- `43b_floor_without_sun_shadows`: disabling the sun's shadows removed the lines entirely. The floor remained unusually dark and visually flat.
- `44_shadow_bias`: changing Light bias alone did not help. URP was using pipeline settings because the additional-light component was absent.
- `45_effective_shadow_bias`: adding the component, disabling pipeline bias, and applying 0.65/0.45 still did not remove the pattern.
- **`46_ceiling_isolation`: disabling only the imported ceiling removed the repeated dashed grid. Broad structural shadows remained.**

Therefore the repeated dashed pattern is associated with the **coffered ceiling casting its pattern onto the floor**, not physical gaps between the floor modules. The waffle ceiling appears to need a proper opaque roof/backing and considered lighting; that is a hypothesis for the next fix, not yet implemented. Do not just remove all shadows permanently. Broad intentional architectural shadows and prop contact shadows matter to the target.

Read-only mesh audit confirmed floor modules have upward world normals `(0,1,0)`, UVs covering 0–1, and 3×3 m bounds. The floor material is `MI_Floor_Tiles_Painted`, with the expected `T_Floor_Tiles_BaseColor` map. The dark overall floor response remains to be assessed after restoring the old floor.
