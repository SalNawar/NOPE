# Live visual review — 2026-10-04

This revision follows Saleh's screenshot of broken floor perspective, broad light columns, missing shadows, undersized characters and identical poses. It supersedes the inclined textured floor in the first completion pass.

The floor continuation now shares the registered painting's transform. Its fourteen grout intersections and individual tangents are traced from the original 2172×724 foreground platform; the hand-painted joints do not have one mathematically exact vanishing point. Horizontal rows use perspective spacing. The first few pixels sample the lit edge directly, then blend to an averaged terracotta colour with gentle surface variation. There is one tile grid. `PaintedReference.png` is an unchanged copy of the approved reconstruction for the lighting-off fallback. The old generated tile texture is retained as reference; its competing grout is no longer drawn.

Window lights are restrained to .055 full strength (.035 for rear windows), with wider falloff, no volume contribution and no low-sun length inflation. Ceiling fixture strength is capped at .18. Existing day-cycle and portal behaviour remain. The directional light and desk lamp now cast soft shadows; the desk's illustrated meshes cast and receive them. Nine soft contact patches register furniture, structural bases and portal bays to the painted floor, supplementing the two existing pier ShadowCaster2Ds. These are illustration contact shadows, not a reconstruction of the entire hall as 3D geometry.

The traveller anchor moves from z=1.6 to z=.55, at the counter, and configured height increases from 1.8 to 1.9 metres. The Back button sits at the upper left so it cannot cover the closer face. World height, feet, click box and shoulder anchor continue to use the existing character canvas.

`LookSpriteStack` gives each traveller one of five deterministic resting stances, plus happy/angry/worried gestures, gentle breathing and head movement. Every body/clothing/face/accessory layer receives the same canvas deformation; lower garments remain outside the arm region. A Jacobian inversion prevents the texture folds found in the first experimental rig. Reduced motion removes ambient movement. Passport stacks retain their original material and crops. This is conservative animation of the delivered frontal artwork, rather than newly drawn side views or dramatic folded-arm poses. The 944 original sprite files and metadata remain unchanged.
