# New hall: panoramic composition study

Replaces the rejected hall design direction; does not replace any Unity assets yet. The existing desk and PC remain separate and unchanged.

One continuous main floor extends from camera to the three ground portals. The rear pair shares a depth and orientation. Upper galleries and the crossbridge use one consistent upper elevation, connected to enclosed circulation cores. Displays belong in recessed bays and the window promenade stays clear.

The leftward pan requires a panoramic master, not stretching a single screen. Reserve a city-dominated left view and a hall-dominated starting view on the right. Final camera field of view and pan angle must be tested in Unity before accepting crop limits; this concept does not prove camera registration.

For production, derive registered hall foreground, window framing/glass, city, floor, fixtures, portal frames, signage, flags and crowd layers. Paint the hidden regions needed for parallax; a simple cut will leave holes. City needs overscan behind mullions. Gameplay text/emblems and portal effects remain independent. Claude owns gameplay hooks.

Lighting: these are composition concepts, not verified albedo maps. Remove residual specular shading/cast light before production, then validate normal/occlusion/emission layers under runtime lighting. Do not call a tinted daylight painting a dynamic lighting implementation.
