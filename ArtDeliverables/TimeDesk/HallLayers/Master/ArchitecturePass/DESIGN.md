# Functional architecture and interior pass

Approved intent: a civilisation once conquered time. Its ceremonial departure terminal now serves a corrupt, overgrown bureaucracy. Monumental order survives under mismatched maintenance and utilitarian retrofits. This pass is a fictional spatial design and image brief; dimensions are working design assumptions, not structural calculations.

## Architectural organisation

Working envelope 48 m wide x 72 m deep. Paired pier lines at x=8 and x=40 m, repeated along the hall. Central span carried by expressed deep steel/composite transfer members, not unsupported stone decoration. Outer bays contain circulation, service cores, lockers and displays. The city curtain wall carries no bridge load. Major piers continue through gallery level; upper supports align with lower supports.

Levels: main departure floor 0; arrival/checkpoint terrace +3 m; upper departure gallery and crossbridge +9 m. Camera is on the front checkpoint terrace. Building arrival lifts and queue are behind the camera/left return, not invented in the portal approach. One desk visible. Screen landmarks and actual circulation must agree.

Traveller route: building lifts → arrival queue → desk → controlled release → left transfer stair or accessible lift → main departure floor → assigned portal. Upper departures use the same lift/stair core to +9 m, then skybridge to the right upper gallery. Staff/service access uses the right core and perimeter corridors. No itinerary requires crossing a portal's maintenance zone.

Skybridge purpose: accessible cross-concourse route from left stair/lift landing to upper gates 04–05 and the right stair/lift core. It has visible framed openings/landings at BOTH ends, clear headroom, continuous parapets and an expressed deep underside beam. Connect to solid cores/piers, never glazing. Reserve the landing at each end; no locker or artifact blocks it.

Three ground portals: 01 at (24,29); 02 at (16,44); 03 at (32,44). Rear pair is symmetrical about the central axis and at identical floor level. Nominal 8 m platform diameters leave generous circulation between them. Bridge crosses farther back near y=54 so its support/landing does not intersect portal 02. Upper 04/05 occupy consecutive right gallery bays. All five apertures remain empty; VFX is deferred.

Keep a clear central approach and continuous perimeter routes. Main floor, platform lips, stairs, pier bases and equipment contact points must be drawn from one perspective construction. Space behind portal 01 must remain visible. No floating rings, plinths or crowd feet.

## Interior decorator pass

- Symmetry belongs to structure, portal spacing, paired banners, lamp mounting heights, floor border alignment and repeated bay framing.
- Functional variation sits inside that order: lockers on left, display/service bays on right. Avoid mechanically mirroring unrelated functions.
- One wall-light family. Mount on matching pier faces at matching heights relative to each floor; size reduces only with perspective. Remove random standing lamps and chandeliers.
- Futuristic lockers recessed flush below left terrace: mixed small valuables, medium bags, tall cases and wide luggage bays, regular outer frame and aligned access controls. Do not reduce landing/aisle width.
- Artefacts are an institutional collection, not random floor props: one neglected painting; astrolabe in secured glass case; ceramic in wall vitrine; relief fixed to rear pier; small sculpture on a restrained display plinth. Position cases inside recessed display bays, clear of stairs and exits, with consistent datum lines.
- Departure board hangs on paired supports on axis and lists 01–05. Signage sits only at route decisions. All signs/emblems are future editable overlays.
- Services are continuous: pier conduits connect to floor trenches and portal bases. Access hatches have working clearance. No isolated decorative cable ends.
- Neglect follows use: worn door pulls, chipped corners, scuffed routes, repaired tile strips, dusty protected displays and inconsistent replacement panels. No uniform grunge blanket.

## Image and lighting requirements

Keep established desk and PC composition. Complete master first, then derive registered layers. Neutral diffuse colour, no directional sunlight bands, cast window shadows, illuminated fixtures, reflections or portal emission. Local form shading in the concept still requires a later albedo review; do not label the generated master production-ready neutral albedo.
