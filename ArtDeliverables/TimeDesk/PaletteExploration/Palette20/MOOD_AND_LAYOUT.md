## User-confirmed setting

- An ill-maintained government building in a harsh future.
- People travel into the past through a programme presented as an opportunity to pay their debts.
- They have no real choice.
- The building should look nice on the surface, but feel disturbing.

## Visual interpretation

Reassuring institutional colours and optimistic messaging coexist with selective neglect. Maintain the portal and account equipment; show sparse, simple repair shapes on architecture. The ordinary desk has been cared for by a person. Keep clean anime/cel rendering, varied plausible material colours, transparent glass, smooth walnut, original floor and indistinct crowds. No global blue wash, green finishes, heavy grunge or photoreal detail.

The poster wording, account numbers and civic emblem are proposed visual storytelling, not approved game lore or implemented mechanics. This mock-up does not establish fees, penalties, travel mechanics, employment terms or interface behaviour.
