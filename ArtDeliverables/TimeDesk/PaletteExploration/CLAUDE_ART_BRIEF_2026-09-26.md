## Setting and emotional target

This is an ill-maintained government building in a harsh future. Travel to the past is advertised as an opportunity to pay off debt. Applicants have no real choice. The building must look attractive and reassuring on the surface, then quietly disturbing on closer inspection: coercion presented as opportunity.

Keep clean, soft anime/cel shading suitable for close desk viewing. Show past institutional ambition, selective neglect and compulsory processing. Optimistic signage, anonymous queues and a well-maintained portal contrast with sparsely patched public infrastructure. Avoid a ruined, uniformly dirty or uniformly dark scene. The colourful desk can show ordinary human care.

## Art constraints

- Use Palette20/palette-20.json as the twenty-colour material guide, not an indexed-pixel limit or blanket scene tint.
- No green, teal, olive, sage or mint on the revised environment and props. Avoid repeating blue across unrelated objects.
- Plain burgundy office pad; coral phone; aubergine till; lavender document tray; mustard pen cup; charcoal lamp and NEXT housing; pale-gold flags; coherent aluminium portal ring with blue energy.
- Preserve material identity. Use neutral metal, plausible plastic/enamel, warm paper and smooth walnut boards with barely visible grain.
- Keep buildings independently coloured and glass visibly transparent. Crowds remain indistinct blended groups at room-appropriate scale, with full legs and no individual characteristics.
- Flags remain actual 2D sprites, not modelled cloth. Keep the restored original floor, room geometry and existing ceiling state.
- Wear is sparse, deliberate cel-painted repair shapes, not photographic grunge or noisy textures.
