## Material and mood rules

- No green, teal, jade, olive, sage or mint on the revised office walls and props. Avoid making unrelated objects the same blue or neutral.
- Apply the twenty material-assigned base colours in Palette20/palette-20.json subtly. The latest PC exception overrides its older proposed porcelain-PC assignment.
- Objects retain plausible material identity: glass transmits, metal stays coherent, separate buildings have varied brick/stone/concrete colours.
- Clean soft anime/cel shading, broad smooth walnut boards and barely visible grain. Plain office pad, no repair grid.
- Preserve the original floor and room geometry. Flags stay actual 2D sprites.
- Crowds read as anonymous overlapping groups with full legs and room-appropriate scale. Keep the teleporter clear.

The user-confirmed setting is an ill-maintained government building in a harsh future. Travel to the past is presented as an opportunity to pay debt; applicants have no real choice. Attractive institutional optimism should conceal coercion and selective neglect. Do not flatten everything into dull colours to express dystopia.
