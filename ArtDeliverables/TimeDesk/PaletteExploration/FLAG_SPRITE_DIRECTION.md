# Flags: 2D sprites

Saleh confirmed the flags will be 2D sprites. Preview them as flat illustrated rectangular banner artwork in their existing positions, with simple shapes and at most one authored flat shade. Do not design them as detailed modelled cloth or simulated fabric. No green. Working palette accent is muted ochre, separate from lamp and till colours. No invented logos; final markings are not specified yet.

This is a rendering constraint for palette previews; no sprite asset import or scene replacement has happened.
