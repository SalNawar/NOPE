# Stylised palette studies — 2026-09-25

The palette guides compatible colours; it is not a recolouring map. Start with the object's material and a plausible natural or manufactured finish, then balance it against neighbouring objects. Keep simple anime cel shading and readable material identity. Use restrained complementary accents on plausible plastic, enamel, leather and fabric, not all architecture.

Glass stays transparent with simple reflection marks; city buildings retain varied natural brick, stone, painted masonry and concrete colours. Floor remains warm stone, visible ceiling cooler plaster. Crowds use separate subdued depth tones. The teleporter retains a coherent metal rim and one teal energy colour. No global tint or rainbow machinery.

Presentation: flat swatches and tiny 2D cel-shaded material samples. No high-fidelity office mockups. Only spare graphic texture marks. Pad colours vary by study; material construction still reads. No Unity assets changed. Character generation stays paused until office art direction is resolved.
